How it holds

Built the slow way, on purpose.

Every Locked Away vault is welded, not bolted together, then hand-fitted so the door seats without a gap you could slide a card through. We test each unit before it ships. We test it again after.

  • Relocking bolts — a drilled lock triggers a secondary bolt system that seizes the door shut permanently. There is no undoing it, by design.
  • UL-rated steel — 11-gauge body, 7-gauge door, poured composite fill.
  • Tamper log — mechanical dials record the last 40 open attempts, visible only to you. Owners tell us they check it more than the contents.
  • Silent seal — no click, no clunk. Some owners tell us they forget what's inside within a year.

Questions

Before you call

What if I forget my combination?

Every Sovereign ships with a registered override key held in a sealed envelope. Call our support line with your serial number and we'll walk you through it.

Can I store correspondence I haven't opened?

Yes. Many clients store mail, contracts, and decision letters here for years without opening them. It's not our business to ask which. It's ours to keep it dry, intact, and exactly where you left it.

Are the vaults fireproof or fire-resistant?

Fire-resistant, rated to the time and temperature listed per model. No commercial safe is permanently fireproof in an unlimited burn.

What happens to items that are never retrieved?

Per estate policy, contents remain sealed indefinitely unless a key-holder requests access. Some units on record haven't been opened since intake. We consider that a feature, not an oversight.

Do you offer international shipping?

Currently we ship within the continental US and Canada. Reach out for freight quotes on estate or business orders.
