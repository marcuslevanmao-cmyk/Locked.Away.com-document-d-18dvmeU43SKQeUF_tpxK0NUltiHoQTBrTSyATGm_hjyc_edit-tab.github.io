How it holds

Built the slow way, on purpose.

Every Locked Away vault is welded, not bolted together, then hand-fitted so the door seats without a gap you could slide a card through. We test each unit before it ships. We test it again after.

  • Relocking bolts — a drilled lock triggers a secondary bolt system that seizes the door shut permanently.
  • UL-rated steel — 11-gauge body, 7-gauge door, poured composite fill.
  • Tamper log — mechanical dials record the last 40 open attempts, visible only to you.
  • Quiet hinge system — no click, no clunk. Some owners tell us they forget it's there.

Questions

Before you call

What if I forget my combination?

Every Keeper ships with a registered override key held in a sealed envelope. Call our support line with your serial number and we'll walk you through it.

Can I bolt a vault to an upper floor?

Yes, with our joist-rated anchor kit. We recommend a licensed contractor confirm load capacity first.

Are the vaults fireproof or fire-resistant?

Fire-resistant, rated to the time and temperature listed per model. No commercial safe is permanently fireproof in an unlimited burn.

Can I put a safe inside a safe?

We advise against nested configurations. Following the incident logged under Vault 07, internal policy requires single-chamber use only for units sold after that date.

Do you offer international shipping?

Currently we ship within the continental US and Canada. Reach out for freight quotes on estate or business orders.
